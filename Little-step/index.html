Questions sponsors ask

Why is it only 5 USD?

5 USD (7,250 RWF) covers one child's school feeding for the full year of three terms. You can also pay for one term only, about 2,400 RWF.

Is my whole gift used for the children?

Yes. 100% of sponsor gifts goes to school feeding. STEP UP HUB members cover the website and running costs.

How will I know my gift helped?

We send a short update with photos or video each term, plus a report that shows how much was received and paid to the schools.

Is it safe for the children?

We only share a child's photo or story with the guardian's written consent, and we never share home addresses.

Can my church or business sponsor many children?

Yes. Use the slider, or contact us to sponsor a whole class.
